## June 12 -rolling updates and rollback

kubectl set image triggers rolling update, zero downtime
watched old pods terminate as new pods came up, never both down
pushed intentionally broken code - CI lint caught it (E999 SyntaxError)
broken image never reached GHCR or k8s - CI is the first safety net
kubectl rollout undo - instant rollback to previous revision
kubectl rollout hisory shos revision numbers
two safety layers: CI prevents bad code, rollback fixes bad deploys
